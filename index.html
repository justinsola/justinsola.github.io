Justin Lucas Sola, PhD

Assistant Professor at UNC–Chapel Hill, jointly appointed in Sociology and the School of Data and Information Sciences.
Email jlsola@unc.edu ORCID 0000-0002-0955-1031

I research how people experience neighborhoods, the criminal justice system, gun ownership, and inequality. I use experiments, machine learning, longitudinal designs, interviews, and observations.

Teaching: sociology · criminology · computational social science · ethics in machine learning
